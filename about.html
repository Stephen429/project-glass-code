About Us

Dedicated to serving the student body of Lydia D. Villangca Trade School through transparent leadership, accountable governance, and meaningful student participation.

The Preamble

"We, the learners of Lydia D. Villangca Trade School, imploring the aid of Divine Providence, believing in the need for a better organized Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) and in the development of the learners as future leaders of the nation, do hereby promulgate and adopt this Constitution and By-Laws of the Supreme Secondary Learner Government for Secondary Schools that shall advance, implement, and maintain our goals and aspirations, embody the ideals and principles of freedom, equality, justice, and democracy, and promote the welfare of all students and the academic standards of our Alma Mater."

Vision

To be a proactive and transparent student government that empowers every learner to achieve their full potential while fostering integrity, participation, and excellence.

Mission

To uphold student welfare through responsible leadership, innovative programs, collaborative initiatives, and accountable governance that positively impacts every Lydian.

About Project GLASS

Project GLASS (Governance, Leadership, Accountability & Student Services) is the official digital transparency initiative of the LDVTS Supreme Secondary Learner Government. The platform centralizes public documents, student-led initiatives, officer information, reports, and other governance resources in one accessible location, strengthening transparency, accountability, and community engagement throughout the school.

Disclaimer

Project GLASS is an independent digital initiative of the LDVTS Supreme Secondary Learner Government. Information published through this platform is intended to promote transparency, accountability, and student access to public organizational records.

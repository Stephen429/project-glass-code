SSLG Passes Policy on School Uniforms, IDs, and Grooming

Written By: SSLG PubCom

Written On: July 26, 2026

SAN RAFAEL, BULACAN — The Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) of Lydia D. Villangca Trade School has officially approved Resolution No. 002, Series of 2026, rolling out updated student guidelines on uniforms, IDs, personal grooming, and routine campus checks. These rules are set to keep our campus neat, safe, and organized. All provisions will strictly take effect immediately.

Key Guidelines to Remember

  • Students must wear their complete, prescribed uniform on regular school days. On Fridays, students are allowed to wear official shirts from active campus clubs recognized this school year.

    Tattered pants, fitted jeans, wide-leg trousers, gray pants, and old or previous years' event/club shirts are strictly prohibited inside the campus.

  • Your official school ID must be worn visibly at all times while on campus grounds. Swapping IDs, or modifying them, which includes covering your photo is strictly prohibited. Lost or damaged IDs must be reported right away for replacement.
  • Students are expected to keep their hairstyles clean, neat, and presentable at all times to maintain a well-groomed look across campus.
  • To keep everyone on track, SSLG officers and assigned school personnel will conduct routine checks during recess and lunch breaks.

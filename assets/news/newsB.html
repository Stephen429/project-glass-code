New School Safety & Dress Code Guidelines

SSLG PMC • 2026-07-05

A safe, organized, and welcoming school doesn't just happen—it takes all of us working together. To make sure our daily routines run as smoothly as possible, the School Governance Council (SGC), and the Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) met on Friday, July 3, 2026. Following that meeting, we have updated two key policies regarding campus safety and student discipline. Please take a few moments to review these important changes below.

1. New Drop-Off and Pick-Up Zones

(Starting Monday, July 6, 2026)

To beat the traffic rush and keep everyone safe during peak hours, we are changing how vehicles drop off and pick up students. We kindly ask all parents, guardians, motorists, and students to follow these new rules:

  • Where to stop: All loading and unloading must happen only at the entrance of the designated pathway.
  • Keep the main gate clear: For everyone's safety, please do not stop right in front of the school's main gate.
  • Vehicles included: This applies to absolutely all rides—private cars, school services, motorcycles, tricycles, and PUVs. By keeping the main gate clear, we can prevent traffic jams and make arriving at and leaving school much safer for our learners.

2. Allowed Dress Code Every Fridays

(Starting Friday, July 10, 2026)

Fridays are a great day to show our spirit while still looking neat and ready to learn. Please make sure your Friday outfits follow these updated guidelines:

✓ What to Wear:

  • Official Club Shirts (created this School Year)
  • Plain White Shirts
  • Maong (Denim) Pants
  • Straight-Cut Pants or Trousers

✗ What NOT to Wear:

  • Baggy or Loose-Fit Pants
  • Skinny Pants

Following these simple outfit choices is a great way to show respect for ourselves and our school community.

Every change we make is with the safety and well-being of our Lydians in mind. Thank you so much to our parents, guardians, and students for always stepping up, cooperating, and supporting our school.

"Together, let us build a safer and more disciplined school environment for every Lydian."
